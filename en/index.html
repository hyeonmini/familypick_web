Common questions

Are my child's photos ever uploaded to a server or external AI?

Never. All photo analysis, Apple Vision matching, and taste learning happen 100% locally on your iPhone. Zero photos or facial vectors ever leave your device.

Are original capture dates preserved when uploading to FamilyAlbum?

Yes. FamilyPick links your original Apple Photos assets only when you tap 'Create Album', keeping 100% of EXIF capture dates and times intact.

Does deleting a temporary album affect my original photos? Is it free?

Your original Camera Roll photos stay 100% untouched — only the temporary album container is removed, and already-albumed photos won't repeat. FamilyPick is free on the App Store.
