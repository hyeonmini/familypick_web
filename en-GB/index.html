04  /  Three Common Questions Q & A
Are my child's photos or videos ever uploaded to a server or cloud AI?+

Never. All photo analysis, local video keyframe checks, Apple Vision matching, and preference learning happen 100% locally on your iPhone.

Are videos supported too, and are original capture dates preserved?+

Yes. Videos stay in Review so you can listen first before ticking ✓, and both photos and videos keep 100% of original EXIF timestamps in the album.

Does removing a temporary album delete originals? Can I re-pick from a past period?+

Only the temporary album list is removed — Camera Roll originals remain untouched. Already-albumed photos stay skipped by default, and you can release that filter for just the period you're viewing. FamilyPick is free.
