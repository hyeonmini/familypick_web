FamilyPick Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026
Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Privacy at a Glance — Clear Two-Layer Architecture
  • Photos, Face/Scene AI & Personal Learning (100% On-Device · Zero Server Collection): Your photos, videos, Apple Vision face and scene feature embeddings, photo capture metadata, filenames/asset IDs, and check (✓) / exclude preference models are processed and stored exclusively on your device. They are NEVER transmitted to Studio Taeo, Amplitude, or any third party.
  • Anonymous Product Interaction Analytics (Amplitude, Inc., USA): To measure app reliability and improve usability, the App sends anonymous, non-linked product interaction events (such as screen views and scan/album action counts under a random anonymous installation UUID — with zero photos, zero biometrics, zero user accounts, and zero IDFA/ad tracking, NSPrivacyTracking = false).

2. Categories of Data Processed & Collected

2.1 Photos, Face/Scene AI & Personal Learning (100% On-Device — Never Collected on Servers)

FamilyPick requires no user account, sign-up, or login. All photo curation and personal preference learning run strictly locally on your iPhone or iPad via Apple's system frameworks (PhotoKit and Vision) and never leave your device:

On-Device Data Category Local Purpose Transmitted Off-Device?
Photo Library images/videos, local asset identifiers, filenames, album titles, and capture metadata (including EXIF DateTimeOriginal and dimensions) Scan your selected date range, filter blur/bursts, and create optional Apple Photos albums preserving original capture timestamps Never (100% On-Device)
Apple Vision face, expression, composition, and scene feature embeddings (numerical feature prints) Match reference photos of your child and distinguish clear child portraits from cluttered rooms or parent-dominated selfies Never (100% On-Device)
Personalized check (✓) / exclude taste weights and previous album inclusion history (ShareHistory) Learn your personal selection style and automatically avoid re-suggesting photos already added to earlier FamilyPick albums Never (Stored solely in local sandbox / UserDefaults)

2.2 Notice Regarding Facial Feature Embeddings & Biometric Privacy Laws (Including BIPA)

To recognize your child across candidate photos on your phone, the App uses Apple's built-in on-device Vision framework to compute numerical visual and facial feature prints locally.

  • Zero Possession or Collection by Studio Taeo: Neither Studio Taeo nor any third-party processor ever collects, captures, purchases, receives, accesses, stores, leases, sells, or profits from any biometric identifiers or biometric information (including facial geometry or faceprints) within the meaning of the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14/) or any other biometric privacy statute. All vectors remain solely inside the private app sandbox on your own device under your exclusive possession and control.
  • Zero External AI Training: Your photos and on-device feature vectors are never uploaded or used to train any external AI or machine-learning model.
  • Immediate Local Destruction: All local feature vectors and learned weights are permanently erased immediately when you tap Reset Learned Preferences in the App's Settings or uninstall the App.

2.3 Anonymous Product Interaction Analytics Collected Automatically (Amplitude)

Solely to monitor stability, measure scan performance, and improve usability, the App transmits anonymous, non-linked product interaction events to Amplitude, Inc. (USA):

Data Type Purpose Strictly Excluded Data
Random locally generated anonymous installation UUID (device_id), session timestamp (session_id), and per-event deduplication UUID (insert_id) Distinguish anonymous app sessions without user accounts or cross-app identifiers Zero photos or thumbnails, zero Apple Vision vectors/biometrics, zero photo asset IDs, filenames, EXIF capture dates, or album titles, zero names/emails, zero Apple IDFA, and zero GPS or precise location data
Non-personal interaction events and aggregate counts (screen views, period preset selection, filter switches, scan completion/pause counts and duration in ms, photo check/uncheck counts, album creation/cleanup counts, partner-app or Share Sheet open actions, and settings/reset actions); app version, OS version, device model, app language, and coarse country/region inferred from IP address upon receipt Measure reliability, diagnose bottlenecks, and improve product usability via Amplitude

Only if you voluntarily send an email to hello@studiotaeo.com or privacy@studiotaeo.com do we receive your email address and message content, which we use solely to respond to and resolve your inquiry.

4. Local Data Control, Retention & Deletion

  • On-Device Taste Learning & Album History: Stored solely on your device. In the App's Settings, you can independently run Reset Learned Preferences or Reset Previous Album History at any time. Uninstalling FamilyPick immediately deletes all local sandbox data, including the random anonymous analytics device_id and any queued events.
  • Candidate Albums in Apple Photos: FamilyPick never creates albums automatically in the background. An album is created in Apple Photos only when you explicitly tap "Create Album" (앨범 만들기). Deleting a candidate album (via FamilyPick's Managed Albums screen or in Apple Photos) removes only the album container and never deletes your original camera roll photos.
  • Photo Library Permissions: You can change or revoke Photo Library access anytime in iOS Settings > Apps > FamilyPick > Photos.
  • Anonymous Analytics Events (Amplitude): Uninstalling the App erases the local random device_id, unlinking any future installation. Events sent to Amplitude are retained under Amplitude's standard retention settings; for privacy inquiries or requests regarding analytics data, contact privacy@studiotaeo.com.
  • Support Emails: Retained only as long as needed to resolve your inquiry and deleted promptly thereafter, unless retention is required by law.

5. Data Sharing, Processors & International Transfers

Studio Taeo does not sell your personal information or share data for cross-context behavioral advertising. We use the following service provider solely for anonymous product analytics:

Processor Service Location Transfer Timing & Method

8. Children's Privacy

FamilyPick is a utility intended for parents and adult guardians organizing family photos. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (US COPPA), under 14 (Korea PIPA), or under 16 (EU/UK GDPR). If we learn that a child has submitted personal information to us by email without parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

9. Data Security

  1. Strict On-Device Isolation for Photos: Keeping photos and Vision embeddings strictly on-device eliminates server breach risks for your photo library.
  2. Transport Security & Schema Enforcement: Anonymous analytics batches are transmitted exclusively over TLS/HTTPS using a strongly typed event schema that forbids photo identifiers, filenames, album names, and personal identifiers.
  3. Sandbox & Support Controls: Local app data is protected by iOS/iPadOS sandboxing and device encryption, and support email accounts enforce multi-factor authentication.
